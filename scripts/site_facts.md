## What the site is
- Free PDF tools, ad-supported (Google AdSense, which uses cookies; never claim "no cookies"). No account, no signup, no email, no watermarks, no daily limits.
- Tools, and only these: Compress PDF (/compress-pdf), Merge PDF (/merge-pdf), Split PDF (/split-pdf), PDF to Word (/pdf-to-word), Remove PDF Password (/remove-pdf-password, for files you own).
- All five tools run entirely in the browser: files are not uploaded to a server.
- There is NO OCR, no PDF-to-image/PNG/JPG converter, no image optimizer, no e-signature, no editing, no annotation, no form filling, no conversion to PowerPoint/Excel on this site. Articles may explain how to do those things with other software, but never as a feature of this site.
- Compress PDF has three levels: Low (best quality), Medium (balanced), High (smallest file, reduced quality). It lowers the resolution of images and scanned pages; with scanned documents the result is usually an image-only PDF whose text is no longer searchable. Never claim it is lossless or "without losing quality" for scans.

## Competitors
- The only competitor fact verified for this site: Smallpdf's free plan allows 2 tasks per day. Any other specific claim about Smallpdf, iLovePDF, Adobe or others (limits, prices, file sizes, technology) must be removed or made general.

## Claims to reject
- Statistics, percentages or case-study results without a named, checkable source.
- Menu paths, settings or apps that do not exist (for example Paint cannot open PDFs; Adobe Acrobat Reader cannot compress PDFs; macOS Preview's PDF export has no quality slider, only the "Reduce File Size" Quartz filter).
